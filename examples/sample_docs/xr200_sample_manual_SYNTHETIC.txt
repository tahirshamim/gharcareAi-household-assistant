XR-200 REFRIGERATOR - SAMPLE USER MANUAL
SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. This is not a real manufacturer manual and contains no real product specifications.

SAFETY WARNINGS

Read this manual before using the appliance.

If you smell gas or burning, do not switch any electrical device on or off. Open the windows, unplug the appliance only if it is safe to do so, and call a qualified technician.

Never use an extension cord or a multi-plug adapter with this refrigerator. Plug it directly into a dedicated, earthed wall socket.

Do not use the appliance if the power cord is damaged. A damaged cord must be replaced by an authorised service centre.

Never spray water on the back or the electrical parts of the refrigerator.
INSTALLATION

Choose a dry, well ventilated place away from direct sunlight and heat sources such as a stove or radiator.

Leave at least 10 cm of free space between the back of the refrigerator and the wall, and 5 cm on each side, so that air can circulate.

Adjust the front feet until the appliance stands level and firm.

After transport, keep the refrigerator upright and wait 4 hours before plugging it in. This allows the compressor oil to settle.

TEMPERATURE CONTROL

The dial on the top of the fridge compartment has settings from 1 (warmest) to 5 (coldest). For normal use set the dial to 3, which keeps the fridge compartment at about 4 degrees Celsius.

Allow 24 hours after switching on before adjusting the dial again.

DEFROSTING AND ICE BUILD-UP

If ice builds up in the freezer compartment, follow these steps:
1. Switch off the refrigerator and unplug it.
2. Remove all food and keep it in a cool place.
3. Leave the doors open for about 6 hours until the ice has melted. Place towels on the floor to catch the water.
4. Wipe the inside dry and plug the refrigerator back in.
Never use sharp or metal objects to remove ice, and never use a hair dryer or heater near the compartment.

Frequent ice build-up can mean that the door gasket is not sealing properly or that the door is often left open. Check the gasket for gaps.

TROUBLESHOOTING

The refrigerator does not cool: check that the plug is fully inserted, that the dial is not set to 0, and that the ventilation space behind the appliance is not blocked. If the problem continues, contact a service centre.

Water leaking at the bottom of the refrigerator: the drain hole at the back of the fridge compartment may be blocked. Clean the drain hole with a soft cloth. Check also that the refrigerator is level.

Unusual noise: a soft humming and occasional gurgling are normal. A loud rattling usually means the appliance is not level or touching the wall.

Error codes shown on the display:
E1 - Temperature sensor fault in the fridge compartment. Switch off, wait 5 minutes and restart. If E1 returns, call service.
E4 - Defrost sensor fault. The freezer may build up ice. Do not try to repair the sensor yourself. Call an authorised service centre.
E7 - Door open for too long. Close the door fully.
